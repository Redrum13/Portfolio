About me

I studied geography in India, then completed the Erasmus Mundus MSc in Cartography at TU Munich, TU Wien and TU Dresden.

I like maps that show change. My MA dissertation mapped 20 years of urban heat in Pune from Landsat data. My MSc thesis tracked moving star dunes in Namibia with Sentinel-2.

I enjoy the whole process. I prepare the data in GIS or Python, then design the map for print or screen. I am looking for roles in cartography, GIS and geodata.
