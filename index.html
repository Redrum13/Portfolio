About

Where geography meets cartography

Based in Munich · Open to relocation

I am a cartographer with a geographer's background. I studied geography in India, then completed the Erasmus Mundus MSc in Cartography at TU Munich, TU Wien and TU Dresden in 2026.

I am most interested in remote sensing and in showing change over time on a map. My MA dissertation mapped twenty years of urban heat in Pune from Landsat data. My MSc thesis tracked moving star dunes in the Namib Sand Sea with Sentinel-2.

I care about how maps communicate. I enjoy the whole workflow, from processing raw data in GIS and Python to designing the final map for print or screen. I am looking for roles in cartography, GIS and geodata.

Field Notes

Cartographic Approach to Remotely Sensed Star Dunes and Environment Dynamics

TU Munich 2026 Sentinel-2 Remote Sensing Geovisualisation

I built a reproducible Python workflow that extracts dune crestlines from multi-temporal Sentinel-2 imagery of the Namib Sand Sea in Namibia, combined with wind observations. I then tested different ways to map their migration over time, including how to show uncertainty and data gaps honestly. The result is a cartographic framework for reading dynamic desert landscapes.

Supervised by Katharina Anders and Pimmbhattra Maydhisudhiwongs
