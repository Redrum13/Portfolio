How does climate activism fit into democracy?

Democracy includes freedom of assembly, association, religion and speech. It also includes citizenship, voting rights, consent of the governed, protection of life and liberty, and minority rights. This makes activism a cornerstone of democracy.

The climate movement puts pressure on governments and industries to act on climate change. Burning fossil fuels drives rising temperatures and shifting weather, which put people and the environment at risk. Activists such as Greta Thunberg call for climate justice. This means those most responsible for climate change should do the most to fix it.
